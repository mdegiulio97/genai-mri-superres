# Model Card — genai-mri-superres

> Transparency document for the model. It follows the spirit of Mitchell et al., *Model Cards
> for Model Reporting* (FAccT 2019, DOI 10.1145/3287560.3287596).

## Model details
- **Task**: ×2 image-to-image super-resolution of 2D medical images (from 32×32 to 64×64).
- **Architecture**: ESPCN with sub-pixel convolution (Shi et al., CVPR 2016,
  DOI 10.1109/CVPR.2016.207); SRCNN is also available (Dong et al., TPAMI 2016,
  DOI 10.1109/TPAMI.2015.2439281). **LeakyReLU** activations (see the note below).
- **Loss**: L1 (default) or MSE. **Config**: see `scripts/train.py`
  (default 30 epochs, batch=64, lr=1e-3, at most 4000 training samples on CPU).
- **Comparison baseline**: bicubic upsampling.

## Data
- **BloodMNIST** 64×64 (MedMNIST v2, Yang et al., *Scientific Data* 2023,
  DOI 10.1038/s41597-022-01721-8), license **CC BY 4.0**. BloodMNIST contains
  blood-cell microscopy images, not MRI.
- LR/HR pairs are generated by controlled **bicubic degradation**: this is *simulated*
  super-resolution, not super-resolution of real low-resolution acquisitions.
- Benchmark dataset; **not** real clinical imaging. Downloaded by `scripts/download_data.py`.
- The separate real brain MRI track (OpenNeuro ds007116, CC0) is described in
  `report/brain_superres.md`; the metrics below refer to BloodMNIST only.

## Metrics (reproducible, `results/metrics.json`, n_test=1000)
| | SR (ESPCN) | Bicubic |
|---|---|---|
| PSNR (dB) | 36.60 | 34.47 |
| SSIM | 0.962 | 0.951 |
- Gain: **+2.13 dB PSNR**, +0.0114 SSIM.

## Methodological note (documented debugging)
With standard ReLU the network fell into **dying ReLU** (zero residual, so the SR output copied
bicubic). Diagnosis: fraction of zero activations = 1.0. Fix: ReLU → **LeakyReLU** plus a
**regression test** that keeps the bug from coming back.

## Intended use
- **Demonstration and teaching only**.
- **It is NOT a medical device** and must NOT be used to reconstruct images intended for
  diagnosis: super-resolution can **introduce plausible but non-existent details**
  (hallucination), a risk that is particularly serious in clinical settings.

## Limitations and risks
- Simulated (bicubic) degradation ≠ real acquisition degradation.
- Toy dataset → metrics do not transfer to real clinical images.
- PSNR/SSIM do not capture **diagnostic fidelity**: task-based metrics and expert review are
  needed before any real use.
